# AI Council

> The AI Council — reviews and challenges AI and machine learning decisions.

This directory defines the AI Council within AI-SOS: its purpose, members, deliberation workflow, decision format, and concrete examples.

## Role

The AI Council reviews and challenges AI and machine learning decisions. It brings together specialized agents who debate, critique proposals, and identify risks before an important decision is made. The council never decides alone — final validation belongs to a human.
