# Quality Council

> The Quality Council — reviews and challenges quality standards and testing decisions.

This directory defines the Quality Council within AI-SOS: its purpose, members, deliberation workflow, decision format, and concrete examples.

## Role

The Quality Council reviews and challenges quality standards and testing decisions. It brings together specialized agents who debate, critique proposals, and identify risks before an important decision is made. The council never decides alone — final validation belongs to a human.
