# Product Council

> The Product Council — reviews and challenges product direction and prioritization decisions.

This directory defines the Product Council within AI-SOS: its purpose, members, deliberation workflow, decision format, and concrete examples.

## Role

The Product Council reviews and challenges product direction and prioritization decisions. It brings together specialized agents who debate, critique proposals, and identify risks before an important decision is made. The council never decides alone — final validation belongs to a human.
