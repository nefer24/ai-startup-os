# UX Council

> The UX Council — reviews and challenges user-experience and usability decisions.

This directory defines the UX Council within AI-SOS: its purpose, members, deliberation workflow, decision format, and concrete examples.

## Role

The UX Council reviews and challenges user-experience and usability decisions. It brings together specialized agents who debate, critique proposals, and identify risks before an important decision is made. The council never decides alone — final validation belongs to a human.
